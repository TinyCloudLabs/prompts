=== Health Log, first approval ===
Run the commands below in your own terminal, one after the other. You may see one or two browser approval screens. The first, if it opens, signs you in to TinyCloud on this computer, so it lists your whole space. The second lets me log this in your Health Log app, and nothing else. Approve each one that opens. If your browser asks to connect to devices on your network, choose Allow. If you cancel a screen, press Ctrl-C in that terminal and tell me. If the page shows a code instead, don't send it to me: I'll give you a command to run in your own terminal, and you paste the code there.
