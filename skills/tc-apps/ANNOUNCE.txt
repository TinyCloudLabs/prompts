=== Health Log, first approval ===
Run the commands below in your own terminal, one after the other. You may see one or two browser approval screens, one at a time. A screen that asks to sign you in to TinyCloud on this computer comes only if you aren't signed in yet. The other lets me log this in your Health Log app, and nothing else. Approve each one that opens. If your browser asks to connect to devices on your network, choose Allow. If you cancel a screen, tell me. If the terminal does not return after you cancel, press Ctrl-C in that terminal. If the page shows a code instead, don't send it to me: I'll give you a command to run in your own terminal, and you paste the code there.
